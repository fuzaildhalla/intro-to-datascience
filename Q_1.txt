3 Apps I Use Daily and How They Use Data

1. Instagram
Instagram uses data about the posts, reels, and accounts I interact with. It uses this information to show me more content that I may like.

2. Zomato
Zomato uses data about my previous orders and searches. It can use this information to suggest restaurants and food that I may enjoy.

3. Paytm
Paytm uses data about my past transactions and payments. It can use this information to make payments easier and show useful payment reminders or offers.
