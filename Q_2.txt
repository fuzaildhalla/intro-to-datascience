Data Scientist Skills Table

Statistics:
1. Probability
   Real-world example: A company can use probability to predict whether a customer is likely to stop using its service.

2. Hypothesis Testing
   Real-world example: An online shopping website can test whether a new webpage design increases the number of people who buy products.

Programming:
1. Python
   Real-world example: A data scientist can use Python to analyze sales data and find which products are selling the most.

2. SQL
   Real-world example: A data scientist can use SQL to get customer and sales information from a company's database.

Domain Knowledge:
1. Business Knowledge
   Real-world example: A data scientist working for a retail company can understand sales trends and help the company decide which products to promote.

2. Marketing Knowledge
   Real-world example: A data scientist can study customer behavior to find which type of advertisement gets more people to buy a product.
